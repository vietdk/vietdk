[QQ] The draft decree allows wholesalers and distributors to decide prices based on market rules, while retail outlets within systems such as Vietnam National Petroleum Group (Petrolimex) and PetroVietnam Oil Corporation (PV Oil) must not exceed published prices, and independent retailers gain pricing autonomy.[QQ]
[QQ] Vietnam Petroleum Association Chairman Bui Ngoc Bao said past pricing based on seven-day global references caused heavy losses when prices fell, leading to low discounts and supply tension despite adequate availability.[QQ]
[QQ] Under the new mechanism, firms will declare actual costs, including inventory periods of up to 20 days, enabling realistic pricing and reducing the risk of forced losses for wholesalers.[QQ]
[QQ] Mr. Bao said differences in procurement costs will create price variation, pushing companies to compete for retailers and consumers, while profitable operations will support stable discounts across the supply chain.[QQ]
[QQ] Regulators will retain oversight through cost audits and inspections, ensuring transparency while allowing market forces to manage supply, pricing, and competition in the fuel sector.[QQ]
